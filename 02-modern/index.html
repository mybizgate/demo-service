From the founders

We used to spend our evenings typing the same quote.

"Berapa harga untuk rumah saya?" is the right question. For two years we answered it one WhatsApp at a time: ask for the bill, ask for a roof photo, do the maths, send a PDF, wait. Half the people went quiet before we could even visit.

So we built the calculator you see above. Now people arrive at the survey already knowing their system size and payback. We talk about their roof instead of their bill, and our evenings are ours again.

— Farah Hanim & Daniel Wong, co-founders
