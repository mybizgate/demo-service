    Before you ask

    Commercial questions, answered up front.

    What are your payment terms?

    30 days from invoice for maintenance contracts, monthly in arrears. Projects: 30% mobilisation, progress claims, 5% retention released after the defects period.

    What is covered under a comprehensive contract?

    All scheduled labour, consumables and replacement parts up to the agreed cap per item, 24/7 breakdown attendance, quarterly thermal imaging and monthly reports. Major overhauls and capital replacements are quoted separately.

    What is your response time?

    Two hours inside the Klang Valley for contract sites, four hours for Johor and Penang contract sites, next working day for non-contract call-outs.

    Do you work with existing BMS and OEM equipment?

    Yes. We maintain Carrier, Trane, Daikin, York and Hitachi chillers, and integrate with Siemens, Honeywell, Johnson Controls and Schneider BMS. OEM parts are used unless you approve an equivalent.

    Can you join our tender or vendor registration?

    Yes. Request the document pack above. We respond to RFQs within five working days and have registered with over 40 property managers, REITs and government agencies.

    Do you offer warranty on repairs?

    Twelve months on workmanship, OEM warranty on parts, and defects liability of twelve months on projects.
